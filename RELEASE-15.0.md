# Business Control ONE 15 MAX

## Focus
- Control Room: unified daily action queue for sales, follow-ups, delivery, cash and content.
- Business hygiene checks for missing follow-ups, contacts, payment due dates and content plans.
- Project pulse with pipeline and delivery signals.
- System health snapshot: local changes, recovery points, media cache and Meta safety state.
- Meta expiry remains non-blocking for local content/CRM work; publishing requires valid Meta credentials and confirmation.
- Mobile-first control room and lightweight rendering.

## Safety
- No automatic publishing.
- No invented pricing, references, certifications or electrical/revision authorizations.
- Cash and pipeline values are derived only from user-entered data.
