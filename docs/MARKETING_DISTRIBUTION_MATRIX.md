## Content rules

- One source idea can become Post + Reel/Short + carousel + story + search article + B2B variant.
- Use native wording per network; do not copy-paste identical community outreach.
- Never invent price, inventory, references, certifications, case studies or results.
- Every commercial asset needs a measurable path to inquiry/order.
- Prefer real project/product evidence over generic AI imagery.
- Do not optimize only for reach; optimize for qualified actions.
